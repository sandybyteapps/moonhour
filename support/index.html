Why are my times different from another app?

Moonhour calculates everything on your iPhone in each spot's own time zone, and checks the results against the US Naval Observatory: sun times within about a minute, moon times within a few minutes. Other apps may use the phone's time zone for every spot, or different period lengths.

How does the rating work?

Open Settings › How the forecast works. In short, the rating combines the moon phase (55%), the moon's distance (15%), and whether a period falls at sunrise or sunset (30%).

Shooting light

Moonhour offers common presets: big game, migratory birds, sunrise to sunset, civil twilight and UK deer. Rules vary by state, country and season, so always confirm the current regulations where you hunt.

Restore purchases

Go to Settings › Restore Purchases, or tap Restore on the upgrade screen.

Cancel a subscription

Go to Settings › Moonhour Pro › Manage Subscription, or to the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions.
