Hunt & Fish Times

Know when they're moving.

Moonhour shows solunar feeding periods, a daily rating, sun and moon times and legal shooting light for your stands, high seats and lakes. Everything is calculated on your iPhone, so it works without signal.

  • Major and minor feeding periods, to the minute
  • A simple daily rating and a full-season calendar
  • Hunt Mode on your Lock Screen and Apple Watch
  • Spots that keep their own time zones
  • Shooting-light presets, including UK deer
