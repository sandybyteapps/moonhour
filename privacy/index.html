Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Moonhour is made by Sandybyte, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom ("I", "me"). Contact: support@sandybyte.com.

The short version

Moonhour doesn't collect, store or sell your personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What happens on your iPhone

  • Location. If you allow it, Moonhour uses your location on your iPhone to calculate sun, moon and feeding times. It is never sent to me.
  • Spots and settings. These are saved on your iPhone, and in your device backups if you use them. I can't see them.

Maps and place names

To search for places, name your current location and find a spot's time zone, Moonhour uses Apple Maps. Apple receives the search text or the coordinates for that request, and Apple's privacy policy applies. I don't receive or store this information.

Weather (Moonhour Pro)

The Conditions card asks Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for the weather at the spot you're viewing, so that spot's coordinates are sent to Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to that request. I don't receive or store this information.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. I don't receive your payment details.

Contacting support

If you email me, I use your email address and message only to reply. I keep them for up to 2 years, then delete them.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask me to access, correct or delete any personal data I hold about you, which in practice means support emails. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

Moonhour isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

I'll update this page if anything changes, and change the date above.
